Assignment: In-Person User Study and Functionality Review

Overview

The purpose of this assignment is to evaluate the functionality and usability of your generative joke application (Project 1) through direct, in-person observation of real users.


You will conduct three (3) in-person user studies where you observe participants interacting with your application. Your goal is to understand how users navigate, interpret, and use your application.


Important: This assignment evaluates the application’s functionality and user experience, NOT the quality of the jokes. Do not include feedback about whether the jokes are funny, clever, offensive, or enjoyable. Focus only on usability, interaction, and functionality.


You must document your findings in Markdown format and paste your write-up into the submission box. Your report should be clearly organized, structured, and easy to read.



Requirements

You must:



Conduct 3 separate user studies

Conduct each study in person

Actively observe the user while they use your application

Watch the user interact with the application in real time

Write your report in Markdown

Submit it in the Submissions section

Keep your report organized, clearly structured, and readable


Remote studies, recorded sessions, or unobserved usage are not allowed.



What to Record for Each User Study

Create a separate section for each of the 3 users. For each user study, include the following:



1. User Information

Include:



Your relationship to the user

(example: friend, classmate, family member, acquaintance, stranger)

Whether the user has used your app before

(none, minimal, moderate, extensive)

Whether the user has experience with similar applications (if known)



2. Observation Context

Describe the study setup:



Where the study took place

What device was used (laptop, phone, tablet, etc.)

What instructions you gave the user (if any)

What tasks you asked the user to perform, or whether you allowed free exploration



3. Three Things the User Liked (Functionality Only)

List at least three specific positive observations related to usability or functionality.


Examples:



The interface was easy to understand

Buttons were clearly labeled

Navigation was intuitive

The app responded quickly


Do not include statements about joke quality.



4. Three Areas for Improvement

List at least three specific areas where the app could be improved.


Examples:



User was unsure where to click first

A feature was difficult to find

Instructions were unclear

User misunderstood how something worked


Be specific and actionable.



5. Observed Friction or Confusion

Describe any friction you observed, such as:



Hesitation before clicking

Misclicks or incorrect navigation

User confusion

User asking questions

User getting stuck

User not noticing important features


Focus on what you observed.



6. Behavioral Observations

Describe what the user actually did, including:



Where they looked first

What actions they took

Whether they explored or waited for guidance

Whether they missed or ignored features

Whether they hesitated or seemed confident


Focus on objective observations, not just their verbal feedback.



7. User Quotes (Optional but Recommended)

Include 1–3 quotes related to usability or functionality.


Examples:



“I don’t know what this button does.”

“This was easier than I expected.”


Do not include quotes evaluating joke quality.



Final Summary (After All 3 Studies)

After completing all three user studies, write a summary section reflecting on the overall findings from your observations.


In this summary, include:



Things you learned from observing users

Things you found surprising

Patterns you noticed across multiple users

At least three specific improvements you plan to make to your application based on this study


Be specific and connect your planned improvements directly to your observations.
